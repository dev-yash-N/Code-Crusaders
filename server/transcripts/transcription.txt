Patient: Good morning, doctor. I’ve been having a headache and mild fever for the last three days.

Doctor: Good morning. Can you tell me exactly how high your temperature has been?

Patient: It’s been around 100.4°F. The headache is mostly in the front of my head, and sometimes I feel dizzy.

Doctor: Any other symptoms? Cough, sore throat, nausea, or vision changes?

Patient: No cough or sore throat. Just some nausea in the mornings.

Doctor: Alright. Do you have any chronic conditions, like diabetes or hypertension? Are you on any medications currently?

Patient: I have mild hypertension, and I take amlodipine daily. No other medications.

Doctor: Okay. Have you taken any medicines for your current symptoms?

Patient: I took paracetamol yesterday, but it didn’t help much.

Doctor: Got it. I will check your vitals and recommend some tests if needed. I suspect it might be a viral infection, but we’ll rule out other causes. I recommend a complete blood count and maybe a basic metabolic panel.

Patient: Sounds good. Should I continue taking paracetamol?

Doctor: Yes, you can continue for fever, but don’t exceed the recommended dosage. Drink plenty of fluids and rest. I’ll prescribe something for nausea if it persists.

Patient: Alright, thank you.

Doctor: I’ll also schedule a follow-up in three days to check your progress. If your symptoms worsen, especially if fever rises above 102°F or you experience severe dizziness, come back sooner.

Patient: Understood. Thank you, doctor.
